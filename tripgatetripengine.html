Architecture — why it's source-agnostic

One data model that stays the same whether the product comes from a supplier, a partner, or TripGate itself. That's what lets the business model evolve without a rebuild.

Lead → Requirements → Travel products → Pricing → Quote → Booking → Documents → Payments → Support

The Trip Engine. You can change the source of the travel products at any time — Supplier · Partner · TripGate — without changing this architecture.

Partner

Partner ├── Type ├── Contact ├── Products ├── Pricing ├── Availability └── Conditions

Travel Product

Travel Product ├── Hotel ├── Transport ├── Activity ├── Guide ├── Restaurant └── Tour

Tour (source-agnostic)

Tour └── Source ├── Internal → TripGate └── External → Partner

Three phases, one system

  • Phase 1 · Launch — Intermediary
    Supplier → TripGate → Customer. Bring clients, build quotes, coordinate, earn commission.
  • Phase 2 · Growth — Network
    TripGate works directly with its own network of partners.
  • Phase 3 · Maturity — Tour operator
    TripGate builds its own packages, itineraries, pricing and branding.

Build the Trip Engine, not a…

  • Not a CRM
  • Not a booking platform
  • Not an ERP
  • A Trip Engine — a machine from Lead to Support, with a swappable product source.
